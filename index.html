Features

Built for Africa. Made for the street.

Everything a bank does, without the pain of being one.

🇿🇦

WhatsApp Native

No new app to download. ZakaPay lives where South Africans already are — on WhatsApp. Works on any phone, any network. Available in Zulu, Xhosa, Sotho, Shona, Portuguese, and English.

🔗

Blockchain Settlement

Every transaction is confirmed on the Stellar network. 5-second settlement. Fully transparent. Your family gets R990, not R890.

🏪

Spaza Shop Network

Cash in and cash out at 200,000+ spaza shops via the Lesaka/Kazang POS network. The last mile, solved.

PIN Security

4-digit PIN with SHA-256 hashing. 3-attempt lockout. Account freeze on suspicious activity. Your keys, your wallet.

Cross-Border

Send money from South Africa to Tanzania, Malawi, and Zimbabwe. Flat R10 fee. Four times cheaper than Capitec.
