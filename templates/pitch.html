The Problem

Millions of migrant workers in
South Africa are overcharged
to send money home.

Zimbabweans, Somalis, Ethiopians, and Mozambicans working in South Africa pay 8-10% in fees via Western Union or Mukuru when sending money home.
A Somali spaza owner sending R20,000/month home loses R1,500+ in fees every month.
The SADC remittance corridor moves over $3 billion annually — yet the people who need it most pay the highest fees.
Western Union requires an app download, a branch visit, and takes minutes to hours. Not built for the informal economy or the people who power it.
