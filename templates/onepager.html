The Problem

Millions of migrant workers in South Africa — Zimbabweans, Somalis, Ethiopians, Mozambicans — send money home every month. They pay 8-10% in fees via Western Union. A Somali spaza owner sending R20,000/month home loses R1,500+ in fees.

The Solution
Send money via WhatsApp — no app download needed

R50 flat fee to any of 10 African countries
5-second settlement on Stellar blockchain
0% FX markup — Stellar DEX rate, no bank intermediaries
KYC via WhatsApp selfie + ID (FICA-ready)
